Explore Egypt - Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

What we collect

If you create an account (email, Google or Facebook sign-in) we store your email address, display name and profile picture URL provided by the sign-in method. We store the places and hotels you save as favorites and the trips you save. We do not collect payment information.

How we use it

To sign you in, sync your favorites and trips across devices, and run the in-app AI assistant. Messages you send to the AI assistant are sent to Google Gemini to generate answers.

Third parties

Supabase (account and data storage), Google (sign-in and Gemini AI), Facebook (sign-in), Cloudinary (image delivery), OpenStreetMap (maps). Hotel booking links open partner websites (such as Agoda or Booking.com) where you pay directly; we may earn a commission on bookings at no extra cost to you. Those sites have their own policies.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data.

Your rights and data deletion

You can delete your saved data or request full account deletion at any time by emailing miloandbruno.official1@gmail.com. We will delete your data within 30 days.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.
